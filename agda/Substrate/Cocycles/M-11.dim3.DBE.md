## Step 1 — Halt and name the target

Build the F₂³ Hodge-complement identification:

- The **V₄-plane** `⟨e₁, e₂⟩` (2-dim subspace, 4 elements) and the
  **chirality-axis** `⟨e₃⟩` (1-dim subspace, 2 elements) are
  *orthogonal* subspaces of F₂³ under the standard bilinear form.
- Together they span F₂³ as a direct sum: `F₂³ = V₄-plane ⊕ Chirality-axis`.
- The chirality-axis IS the Hodge dual of the V₄-plane at n=3.

This is the **first concrete proof in Agda** of the chirality-as-
Hodge-dual unification (per memory `project_3plus1_parity_universal`).
It demonstrates at the smallest non-degenerate scale (n=3) the
identification that lifts to n=5 (Live/Reserved) and n=8
(ext-Hamming parity).

## Step 2 — Search for repeatable form

Per the 3+1 parity universal pattern (memory), every W ⊕ U
decomposition of F₂^n by a 2-dim V₄ subspace gives **two orthogonal
projections**:

| Side | Projection | Kernel = "the other subspace" |
|------|-----------|-------------------------------|
| V₄ side | `Linear n → 2` (project V₄-coords) | the U subspace (V₄-zero coset) |
| U side | `Linear n → (n−2)` (project U-coords) | the V₄ subspace itself |

At n=3, the "U side" projection has codomain `Linear 3 → 1` (since
n−2 = 1, the chirality bit alone). Orthogonality of the two
subspaces follows from the projections being onto **disjoint
coordinate sets** in the standard basis.

Repeatable form: **complementary kernel-code construction**. This
pattern lifts directly to M-10A (n=5, Reserved-Selector projects
V₄-coords) and to a hypothetical M-11.dim5 (where the U-side
projection would project the 3-dim Axis × Chirality subspace).

Per orbit-saturation discipline (`shadow-architecture` skill rule
6): this is S2G (catalogue instances at each n), NOT RFS (no
wrapper extraction — the generator IS the W ⊕ U decomposition).

F₂³, the subspace `⟨e₁, e₂⟩` and the subspace `⟨e₃⟩` are Hodge
