# DBE: M-11.metric-gauge — 28 non-degenerate bilinear forms + GL(3, F₂) orbit

**Cross-reference:** parent plan at `agda/Substrate/Cocycles.DBE.md`;
memories `project_3plus1_parity_universal`,
`project_reserved_selfdual_bijection_gauge`,
`project_tetrative_metacircularity`. Motivated by external observations
in `scratch/obs-1.md`, `obs-2.md`, `obs-3.md` (specifically the 28-
metric claim verified by hand: 64 symmetric 3×3 matrices over F₂, of
which exactly 28 are non-degenerate).

**The gauge being formalised.** The discrete Hodge star at n=3 needs a
non-degenerate symmetric bilinear form to define orthogonality. Over
F₂, there are exactly **28** such forms (verified). GL(3, F₂) acts on
them by congruence `M ↦ T^T M T`, and the action is **transitive**
(single orbit), with stabiliser of order 168/28 = 6 = |O(3, F₂)|.

This is the **metric gauge** — yet another L-level in the tetrative
tower, sitting between L0 (object) and L2 (bijection): the choice of
which bilinear form to use as "the standard metric" for the Hodge ★.

## Step 2 — Search for repeatable form

The repeatable form: **F₂-linear gauge structure** — a set of
"equivalent choices" that an outer group acts on transitively, with
stabiliser the "structure group." This pattern recurs:

| Gauge | Set | Acting group | Orbit | Stabiliser |
|-------|-----|--------------|-------|-----------|
| Bijection (M-11.dim4.bridge) | 168 Vector 3 ↔ SelfDual | GL(3, F₂) | single | trivial |
| Metric (this slice) | 28 non-deg sym forms | GL(3, F₂) | single | O(3, F₂) = 6 |
| Fano-point (M-11.fano) | 7 Fano points | GL(3, F₂) | single | 168/7 = 24 |
| Chirality-axis (M-11.dim3) | 7 1-dim subspaces | GL(3, F₂) | single | 24 |

These are all `GL(3, F₂)`-orbits of different size; the bound is
|GL(3, F₂)| = 168, with the orbit-stabiliser product = 168 in each
case. The metric gauge slots in as one more instance of this pattern.

Per orbit-saturation: the GL(3, F₂) generator (σ + τ from M-11.fano.gl3)
acts the same way on all these gauges; resist extracting a generic
"F₂-orbit-Gauge" wrapper. The metric instance has its specific
congruence action; that's the content.

## Step 3 — Name the costructure (shadows)

**N-1 — `SymBilinForm-3`** type alias + 6 accessors (a, b, c, d, e, f).
  File: `Substrate/Algebra/F2/HodgeDim3/MetricGauge.agda`.

**N-2 — `det-sym3 : SymBilinForm-3 → F₂`** computing
  `a·b·c + a·f + c·d + e·b`. ~10 LoC.

**N-3 — `NonDegenerate`** predicate + decidability (`det-sym3 m ≡ 𝟙`).

**N-4 — 3 structural-exemplar metrics (NOT enumeration positions)**:

  Three witnesses, chosen as REPRESENTATIVES of structurally distinct
  shape-classes (under the S₃ axis-permutation subgroup of GL(3, F₂);
  under the full GL(3, F₂) they're all gauge-equivalent in the same
  orbit). The point is to expose the SHAPE-CLASSES, not to begin an
  enumeration of 28 forms.

  - `metric-id` = diagonal identity (a=b=c=1, others=0) — the
    "orthogonal" shape-class.
  - `metric-mixed` = diagonal + one off-diagonal coupling (e.g.,
    a=b=c=1, d=1) — the "partially-coupled" shape-class.
  - `metric-fully-coupled` = an example with all couplings non-zero
    — the "fully-coupled" shape-class.

  Each with a `refl`-closing non-degeneracy witness. The 3 are
  exemplars exposing the GL(3, F₂)-orbit's internal shape-class
  structure (per the 1 + 9 + 15 + 3 = 28 classification under axis-
  permutation), not "positions 1, 2, 3 of an enumeration."

**N-5 — `congruence-act : Linear 3 3 → SymBilinForm-3 → SymBilinForm-3`**.
  Computes the new entries (a', b', c', d', e', f') from the action
  T^T M T. ~50 LoC of matrix arithmetic; each new entry is a sum of
  products of T-entries and M-entries.

**N-6 — One concrete transitivity witness**: explicit T ∈ Linear 3 3
  (invertible) with `congruence-act T metric-id ≡ metric-1-interaction`,
  closing by refl after computation.

**N-7 — Documentation** of the 28-orbit + stabiliser structure, the
  GL(3, F₂)-gauge framing, and connection to the metric-choice rung
  on the tetrative tower (this IS another sacrifice rung: sacrificing
  "the standard metric is the diagonal identity" for "any of 28
  metrics is equally valid").

## Step 5 — Entailment

**Entailment claim:** The 28 non-degenerate symmetric bilinear forms
over F₂³ form a SINGLE GL(3, F₂) orbit under congruence, with
stabiliser O(3, F₂) of order 6. The metric choice for the Hodge ★ is
therefore a 28-element gauge, all elements gauge-equivalent.

**BHK content:** N-6's explicit witness T (transitivity from
metric-id to metric-1-interaction) IS the BHK-constructive proof of
ONE step in the orbit. Full transitivity (all pairs) follows from
the theory but requires 378 witnesses; deferred.

**Why this matters:** the existing `_·F_` dot product (from
DotProduct.agda) is the "standard metric" (= the diagonal identity
form). It's ONE of 28 equally-valid choices. Downstream Hodge
constructions should acknowledge the metric gauge rather than
implicitly fixing the standard one.

## Connection to the tetrative tower

This slice adds a new node to the meta-level ladder:

- **L_metric** (new): the 28-element metric gauge, sitting at the same
  meta-level as L2 (bijection gauge) but parametrising the metric
  choice for the Hodge ★ rather than the bridge between Reserved and
  SelfDual.

- **Both L_metric and L2 are GL(3, F₂)-orbits**: 168 = 28 · 6 and
  168 = 168 · 1 respectively. They're "siblings" at the same meta-
  level, related by being different gauges acted on by the same
  symmetry group.

- **Sacrifice rung interpretation**: this is rung "sacrifice the
  standard metric" — the substrate had implicitly committed to the
  diagonal identity as "the" metric (via `_·F_` in DotProduct.agda);
  this slice surfaces that as a gauge choice and admits 27 others.

## Connection to the catalog conjecture

The metric choice affects the Hodge ★ definition and hence the
"chirality dual" structure. With different metrics, "what counts as
self-dual" changes. The 8 self-dual bivectors of the standard Hodge ★
(M-11.dim4.SelfDual) become a DIFFERENT 8-element subspace under a
different metric — but all 28 "8-element self-dual subspaces" are
GL(3, F₂)-orbit-equivalent.

This formalises the catalog's hint that the "Reserved = signed
singletons" identification depends on the metric, and exposes the
metric choice as a first-class gauge alongside the bijection gauge.
