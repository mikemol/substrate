# DBE: M-11.dim4 — Λ²(F₂⁴) bivectors + Hodge ★ for grade 2

**Cross-reference:** parent plan at `agda/Substrate/Cocycles.DBE.md`;
prior slices M-11.dim3 (chirality at p=p₄) and M-11.fano (parametric
at all 7 Fano points). Memories: `project_3plus1_parity_universal`,
`feedback_universal_property_discipline`.

The **medium step** in the chirality-rotation arc: the smallest case
where Hodge ★ has genuine exterior-algebra content (not just
complement-of-1-dim-subspace). In F₂⁴, Hodge ★ at grade 2 is the
**self-dual** map Λ² ↔ Λ² (an involution); its fixed subspace has
8 elements, hinting at connection to the catalog's "8 reserved
signed singletons."

## Step 1 — Halt and name the target

**In scope:**

- `Bivector = Vector 6` as the representation of Λ²(F₂⁴) (dim = C(4,2) = 6).
- Basis 2-blades indexed by Fin 6 corresponding to ordered pairs
  (i, j) with i < j, i, j ∈ Fin 4.
- `complement : Fin 6 → Fin 6` — the "index reversal" map sending each
  basis 2-blade index to its Hodge dual's index. Specifically:
  0 ↔ 5, 1 ↔ 4, 2 ↔ 3.
- `hodge-star : Linear 6 6` — the Hodge ★ as an F₂-linear map, built
  via `linear-from-images (λ i → basis (complement i))`.
- `hodge-involution : ∀ ω → apply hodge-star (apply hodge-star ω) ≡ ω`
  — the ★² = id property, proven via linear-extensionality (M-3.5
  discipline) + `complement (complement i) ≡ i` (6 cases by refl).
- `SelfDual` predicate (and KernelCode form): bivectors fixed by ★.
  The fixed subspace has 2³ = 8 elements.

**Out of scope (deferred):**

- Exterior product ∧ : Λ¹ × Λ¹ → Λ² (would require general wedge
  machinery; not needed for the Hodge ★ at grade 2 alone).
- The connection between Λ² self-dual subspace (8 elements) and
  the catalog's "8 reserved signed singletons" (separate slice
  M-11.dim4.reserved-bridge).
- Λᵏ(F₂^n) for general k, n (full exterior algebra tower; deferred
  to a much larger slice if needed).
- Hodge ★ at grade 1 or 3 in F₂⁴ (Λ¹ ↔ Λ³, both 4-dim, both 16
  elements; deferred unless a consumer needs them).

## Step 2 — Search for repeatable form

The repeatable form: **2-blade index ↔ complement-pair**. Each basis
2-blade `e_i ∧ e_j` (with i < j in Fin 4) has a unique complementary
2-blade `e_k ∧ e_l` (with k < l and {i, j, k, l} = {0, 1, 2, 3}).
The 6 basis 2-blades pair up into 3 complementary pairs.

Under the Fin 6 index encoding (lex-order on (i, j)):

| Fin 6 | Pair | Complement Pair | Fin 6 |
|-------|------|-----------------|-------|
| 0 | (0,1) | (2,3) | 5 |
| 1 | (0,2) | (1,3) | 4 |
| 2 | (0,3) | (1,2) | 3 |

So `complement` on Fin 6 is the **index-reversal involution**:
`complement k = 5 − k`. Three pairs, fixed-point-free, all cases by refl.

Per orbit-saturation discipline: this is a finite-set involution
defined by enumeration (6 cases); the "generator" is the index-pair
complement map; no wrapper extraction.

## Step 3 — Name the costructure (shadows)

**N-1 — `Bivector` and basis 2-blade enumeration**: `Bivector = Vector 6`
  type alias + helper functions to interpret indices as 2-subsets of
  Fin 4 (`pair-from-index : Fin 6 → Fin 4 × Fin 4`, etc.). Documentation
  links each Fin 6 index to its 2-subset.

  File: `Substrate/Algebra/F2/HodgeDim4/Bivector.agda`.

**N-2 — `complement` map + involution**: `complement : Fin 6 → Fin 6`
  via 6-case pattern match (0↔5, 1↔4, 2↔3); proof
  `complement-involution : ∀ i → complement (complement i) ≡ i` by 6 refls.

  File: `Substrate/Algebra/F2/HodgeDim4/Bivector.agda` (same as N-1).

**N-3 — `hodge-star : Linear 6 6`**: built via `linear-from-images`
  with basis-images `λ i → basis (complement i)`. The Hodge ★
  permutes basis 2-blades by sending each to its complement.

  File: `Substrate/Algebra/F2/HodgeDim4/HodgeStar.agda`.

**N-4 — `hodge-involution`**: ★² = id on all bivectors. Proved via
  linear-extensionality (M-3.5 discipline): two Linears agree iff
  they agree on basis. For each basis e_i (i ∈ Fin 6):
  ```
  apply hodge-star (apply hodge-star (basis i))
    ≡ apply hodge-star (basis (complement i))      [by apply-linear-from-images-basis]
    ≡ basis (complement (complement i))            [by apply-linear-from-images-basis]
    ≡ basis i                                      [by complement-involution]
  ```
  6 cases, each ~5 lines of `trans`-chaining. ~50 lines total.

  File: `Substrate/Algebra/F2/HodgeDim4/HodgeStar.agda`.

**N-5 — `SelfDual` predicate + KernelCode**: ω is self-dual iff
  `apply hodge-star ω ≡ ω` iff `apply (hodge-star −L id-L) ω ≡ 𝟎ⱽ`
  (where `−L` is Linear subtraction = sum since we're in F₂). The
  self-dual subspace = `KernelCode 6 6` for the Linear `λ ω →
  apply hodge-star ω +ⱽ ω` (since `+ = −` in F₂).

  The 8 elements: the 3-dim subspace `{(c₀, c₁, c₂, c₂, c₁, c₀) :
  cᵢ ∈ F₂}` (verified by hand-check: any vector of this shape is
  fixed by the index-reversal involution).

  File: `Substrate/Algebra/F2/HodgeDim4/SelfDual.agda`.

## Step 5 — Entailment

**Entailment claim:** the Hodge ★ at grade 2 in F₂⁴ is an F₂-linear
involution; its self-dual subspace has 2³ = 8 elements; this is the
smallest case of Hodge ★ where the operator is non-trivial
(non-identity on basis blades) AND has a non-trivial fixed subspace.

**BHK content:** N-3 IS the operator; N-4 IS the involution proof;
N-5 IS the structural identification of the 8-element fixed subspace.
The catalog's "8 reserved signed singletons" reading lifts (via a
deferred bridge slice) to identifying these 8 self-dual bivectors
with the 8 reserved codewords.

**Why this is mechanical via the discipline:**
- N-3 is a single `linear-from-images` call with the complement map.
- N-4 reduces to `complement-involution` (6 refls) via linear-
  extensionality + `apply-linear-from-images-basis` (the foundational
  primitive added in M-11.fano N-1).
- N-5's KernelCode is `record { parity-check = hodge-star-minus-id }`.

## Connection to the larger conjecture

This slice grounds the **smallest case of Hodge ★ with genuine
exterior-algebra content**. The Hodge ★ at grade 1 in F₂³ (M-11.dim3 /
M-11.fano) reduces to subspace orthogonality; nothing about it
required `Λᵏ` types. At grade 2 in F₂⁴, the ★ is a non-trivial
involution on bivectors — the first case where the exterior algebra
matters operationally.

The 8 self-dual bivectors are a candidate identification for the
catalog's "8 reserved signed singletons." Confirming this would
require a separate bridge slice (M-11.dim4.reserved-bridge) showing
the 8-element subspace structurally matches Codeword.agda's Reserved.
