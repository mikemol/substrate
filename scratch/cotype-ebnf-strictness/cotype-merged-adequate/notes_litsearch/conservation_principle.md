# The Conservation Principle — triviality-out = witness-in (Noether schema)

## Statement (USER's, ring-theory conversation, 2026-03-27)
"Algebraic structure and numeric structure have a conservation law between them; as the feature space
gets richer, the number of trivial laws (assumptions) in the algebra reduce." A trivial law (holds
unconditionally, requires no witness, admits no exception) had been CARRYING information all along,
invisible within the algebra that assumed it away. Releasing it does not break arithmetic — it UNCOVERS
the dimension the law was suppressing. Conserved quantity: count-of-unconditional-assumptions <-> dimension-
of-distinguishable-feature-space.

## The Noether reading (why "loses a law" and "gains structure" are ONE exchange)
A symmetry (trivial law) and a conserved quantity / degree of freedom (feature dimension) are the same
thing read from two ends. Releasing the symmetry IS liberating the dimension. So "Cayley-Dickson loses
laws" and "APSP gains length" are not opposite directions — they are the two sides of one ledger, and any
disanalogy between them is an artifact of reading the law-side of one and the structure-side of the other.

- CAYLEY-DICKSON (dim 2^n): release one classical law per step n=1..4 (reality/self-conjugate, then
  commutativity, associativity, alternativity+division), then STABLE (power-assoc/flexibility/unitality

  Refs: Baez "The Octonions" (the law ladder); Moreno 1998 / Reggiani arXiv:2411.18881 (G2 zero-divisor

- APSP epochal doubling: release LOCALITY per epoch ("is-atomic" stops holding unconditionally); the
  composite segment carries a NEW DECOMPOSITION (child-pair + ancestry/descendant-set) as witness. UNBOUNDED
  reservoir (locality is releasable at every scale). This is the "lost law per epoch" the deep-research
  report said did not exist — it does: it is locality. [The finite-vs-unbounded difference = how much
  triviality each starting object had to spend, NOT a difference in the exchange mechanism.]
- COTYPE two-mismatch: mult = p*C(c,2) - [(p-1)/2 + 1]*(c-1). The correction [(p-1)/2 + 1] is exactly TWO
  witnesses for two released trivialities: (p-1)/2 = the reality involution (released self-conjugate, the
  unsigned-non-chain-map mismatch) + 1 = the frame mode (released equivariance/locality, the non-equivariant

  conservation law instantiated: each mismatch is one released triviality carrying its witness.

## The runtime-distinction criterion is the same principle (USER's standing law)
"valid iff constructible -> reachable -> observable -> coverable" = a PROHIBITION on trivial laws: any law
holding unconditionally collapses a distinction behavior may depend on. A valid distinction is one that

and the cotype two-mismatch are ONE principle: don't assume a law for free; carry its witness; the witness
IS the structure.
