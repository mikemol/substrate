**Fact.** The mechanical CV-joint family is a kinematic instantiation of the substrate's gauge-sacrifice ladder, with **12 family members** organized into **3 Sylow-target classes** matching GL(3, F₂)'s prime factorization 168 = 2³·3·7. **Why:** What began as a single anchor instance ([[thompson-coupling-kinematic-realization]]) has accumulated into a near-exhaustive engineering taxonomy. The catalog now spans heavy industrial (Cornay, Gear), compliant-material (Rag Joint), and modern high-performance (Countertrack/SX) variants. **How to apply:** When discussing gauge-sacrifice or 168-gauge, the catalog gives 12 concrete kinematic anchors organized by Sylow target. When a new mechanism surfaces, classify by Sylow first (which prime-power target field does its symmetry inhabit?), then by sub-pattern (rigid vs compliant; discrete vs continuous; single-orbit vs split vs crossover).

## Recovery beyond equivariance — new sub-patterns

Three sub-patterns surface in the extended catalog that go beyond "sacrifice X to recover homokinetic equivariance" with a richer structural meaning. These are candidate next-level abstractions:

### (a) Continuous-limit DOF (Gear Coupling)

The Gear Coupling shows Sylow-3's discrete cyclic orbit **extending continuously** to S¹. Microscopic point contacts produce an **analog averaging computer** rather than a discrete switching computer.

**Substrate reading**: cyclic structures (Z/n) can have continuous limits (S¹ = lim Z/n as n → ∞). The substrate's [[continuous-via-discrete-inference-rules]] discipline is the right ambient — formalize the **constructor** of the continuous limit (the discrete-to-continuous inference rules), not the limit object itself.

### (b) Compliant recovery (Rag Joint)

The Rag Joint sacrifices rigid mechanics entirely; recovery is via **elastic deformation of material**. "Material as zero-annihilation field" is a structurally distinct recovery mode from rigid kinematic constraint.

**Substrate reading**: alongside discrete kinematic gauge mechanisms, the substrate might admit "elastic" or "absorptive" gauge structures — recovery achieved by integrating over deformations rather than locking exact constraint paths. Connects loosely to [[continuous-via-discrete-inference-rules]] and to the "trade structural DOF for continuous DOF" pattern.

### (c) Sylow crossover (Double Offset)

The DOJ explicitly straddles Sylow-2 and Sylow-3 — a 6-ball cyclic ring glides along axial sign-bit translation. This is the first cataloged case of a single mechanism inhabiting **two Sylow targets simultaneously**.

**Substrate reading**: the FieldFanOut architecture ([[168-tower-as-fanout]]) decomposes 168 into three independent prime-power factors — but real mechanisms can SHARE structure across factors. The crossover-shape mechanism deserves first-class status in the substrate's gauge architecture (perhaps as a `FieldBondCrossover` primitive between two FanOut targets).

### (d) Internal antiphase (Countertrack)

The Countertrack joint introduces an **internal counter-running structure**: 2 alternating orbits of N/2 in geometric antiphase. Cage forces self-cancel internally rather than being externally constrained. This is a **nested gauge** — the joint itself contains a sub-joint with opposing chirality.

**Substrate reading**: this is the kinematic embodiment of the substrate's own [[3plus1-parity-universal]] / chirality-flip pattern at the gauge mechanism's internal level. The Countertrack pattern suggests a primitive `InternalAntiphasePair` that wraps any single-orbit gauge mechanism into a self-cancelling pair.
