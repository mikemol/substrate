1. **Parameterize the held family.** A record/def whose Set-valued field or ∀-over-Set quantifier "IS its
   content" drops when the family becomes a PARAMETER — params never raise the sort (the whole arc's thesis).

2. **Represent the universe generatively (the CF-for-reals move).** "Can't enumerate all Sets/reals" is FALSE

   `El (a ⌜⇒⌝ b) = El a → El b`), not raw `Set`. `ExtrudeIOMonadRecord.Code/El` is the in-repo shape; `Roster.

   function spaces into ℕ, the universe GENERATES them as `⌜⇒⌝` codes. (Set-Category; ⟡pyrig-object-carrier.)
3. **The ∀-token IS the representation** ("this is how knots work"). A universal over a Set-family (IsSimple's
   normal-subgroup ∀, BraidLawType's BitVector ∀) is not a wall — the invariant is its finite presentation;

4. **Inline + delete the alias.** A type-synonym `Rel : Set→Set→Set₁` / `Fam : Set→Set₁` is irreducibly Set₁ as
