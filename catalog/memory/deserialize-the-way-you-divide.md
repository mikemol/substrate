The `open … public` re-export chain that unfolds a heavy proof substrate's whole quotient onto every consumer is the deserialization-floor anti-pattern. The fix is the repo's OWN primitive applied reflexively: **the substrate should deserialize the way it divides.**

**The finding (measured, 2026-07-23, the AES/cap128 arc).** Agda's per-module deserialize floor decomposes above the fixed ~62 MB process baseline as: algebra-record tower (Magma→…→CommutativeRing) = **+5** (cheap — level-poly record discipline); the graded-poly substrate (`Poly` via `Graded.Div`) = **+38**, 55% of it the base `Graded` multiplicative suite (`*P`/convCoeff/nth-*P/basis-decomposition), only 6% the `Div`/EEA layer itself (division is LIGHT — built on the heavy base). The distribution is FLAT (gini 0.283, top-10 defs = 8% of weight) ⟹ **split-by-access-path, NOT seal-the-giants** — there is no sealable monolith. Instrument: catalog.db (per-def `unit_node` COUNT = node count = deserialize-weight proxy), fetched sqlite→numpy; NOT ~150 s agda probes, NOT the struct-only `refs` view (function-level access needs the SPPF term-node walk).

**The either/or collapse (user, verbatim-load-bearing): "route AND seal", "this is why the agda repo has a notion of graded division in the first place."** Neither pure split (fights the dependency DAG — the heavy defs are a TOWER of supersets: `nth-*P ⊃ convCoeff ⊃ *P`) nor pure opaque-seal (the body still deserializes). The move is the wedge `a = recon q b r`:
- **SEAL** the multiplicative quotient `q·b` (the superset proof-tower) behind a graded boundary — a consumer deserializes the STATEMENT-HANDLE (a neutral), not the 10 000-node expansion. "normalize is a hom across the seal."
- **ROUTE** the residue `r` — the small interface a consumer actually names — à la carte.
A GRADED (not flat) division means a consumer at grade k never re-expands grades below k: the seal boundary IS the grade, `recon` is the cross-boundary assembler-as-homomorphism. Graded division was BUILT for exactly this. See [[adjunction-is-the-wedge-residue-counit]] (residue = orientation = counit-defect), [[feedback_opaque_staging_bounds_dense_elaboration]] (seal each stage; normalize distributes), [[feedback_fix_infra_not_route_around]].

**Corollary — a route-around is a diagnostic, not a fix.** Rebasing AES's `Poly 8` byte on raw `Vec F₂ 8` dropped the floor ~100→71 and PROVED the substrate is the cost — but the user rejected shipping it: "the AES proof is only interesting BECAUSE it proves AES in algebraic terms." Fix the infra (make the substrate deserialize cheap via the wedge), don't strip the algebra per-consumer. The raw-Vec probe localized the floor; the wedge-seal is the fix.
