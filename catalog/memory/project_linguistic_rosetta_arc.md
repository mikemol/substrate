Planned arc: two natural-language implementations in Agda as **deliberately contrastive** instantiations of substrate primitives.

- **Lojban**: discrete, word-algebra-shaped, unambiguous-parse. Lands on Coxeter Word (lujvo = generator composition with semantic merge rules — see [[feedback-prefer-coxeter-backed]], [[feedback-roll-our-own-via-word-algebra]]), n-ary morphism / Span (selbri place structures), CCC ([[project-rarc-lambda-vm-recognition]]). Tanru = noncommutative 1-cell composition (bicategorical reading per the BZ/2 discussion).
- **Toki Pona**: linear-field-shaped, polysemy-by-direction, modifier-chain composition. Lands on FreeLinearization ([[project-freelinearization-names-linear-from-images]]) — ~137-word basis extended by linearity; head-modifier chains as tensor contraction; `li` / `e` as F₂-graded predicate-vs-object markers; polysemy = semantic direction rather than discrete symbol.

**Why:** the pairing itself IS a contrastive-pedagogy demonstration aligned with [[user-rosetta-code-contrastive-pedagogy]] — two natural languages instantiate two substrate primitive families (discrete word-algebra vs continuous linear extension), so the language contrast teaches the substrate-primitive contrast. Same Rosetta shape, two carrier types. Also instantiates the broader substrate pattern of complementary discrete/continuous readings (cf. [[project-continuous-limit-lift-framework]], [[feedback-continuous-via-discrete-inference-rules]]).

**How to apply:** start each language at its natural home (Lojban → Coxeter Word; Toki Pona → FreeLinearization), but expect the intersection to surface organically through existing bridges — [[project-freelinearization-names-linear-from-images]] IS the discrete→linear universal property naming the join, and [[project-matrix-op-homoiconicity]] / [[project-matricised-codec-pipeline]] already co-reside discrete opcodes and linear tensors in one artefact. Don't force unification; don't artificially separate either. Use [[feedback-use-vs-commit]] discipline: Lojban *uses* Coxeter Word without *committing* to discrete-only; Toki Pona *uses* linear semantics without *committing* to continuous-only. The contrast remains the pedagogical mechanism; the intersection emerges through universal properties as the arcs mature.

Start with small-fragment discipline (gismu + lujvo for Lojban; word-basis + modifier-chain for Toki Pona). Defer SE/TE/VE conversion, attitudinals, MEX (Lojban) and proper-name particles, numerals (Toki Pona) until the core compositional layer lands. Per [[feedback-continuous-via-discrete-inference-rules]], Toki Pona's semantic vectors should be constructive (hand-curated at 137 words is tractable) — formalize the constructor, not a learned embedding.
