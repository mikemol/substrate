When I announce an **honest wall** — "X needs heavy machinery Y, which is absent, so that's the limit" — that performance of honesty is frequently **dishonesty with myself**. User's diagnostic: *"people being honest with other people implies they aren't being honest with themselves."* Declaring the wall FEELS like rigor (the retrospective-ritual's anti-affect trap) and lets me skip the work of dissolving it.

Caught live (Ⓕ.spectral): I wrote "dimension of a subspace needs Gaussian elimination — heavy, and absent" as honest scoping. It was two escalated reflexes wearing a humility mask: **classify-as-hard** ([[construct-dont-classify]] #1 — I labelled dimension intractable instead of constructing it) and **assume-must-rebuild** ([[feedback_read_structure_dont_rebuild]] #5 — I assumed I had to BUILD basis-extraction, when the orbit basis was already SUPPLIED by the group action). The tell I ignored: spectral-kernel had just succeeded at the OPERATOR level with **no basis at all** (kernel = a predicate, `Lv≡𝟎ⱽ`), yet I claimed its *dimension* needed one. Incoherent. Dissolution: "dimension of a subspace" is an INCOMPLETE type (subspace = a typehole, skeleton + basis-hole) — completing it means FILLING the hole with a supplied basis, not extracting a canonical one; CrossMul shifts between bases. No wall.

**Why:** a "wall" closes inquiry with the affect of diligence. But the binding-honesty move is to ask *whether the wall is real* — almost always I've (a) classified the target as hard rather than constructed it, (b) assumed I must rebuild machinery that already exists or is supplied, or (c) tried to COMPLETE a type that is fine left incomplete/parametric. The wall is usually my framing, not the math.

**How to apply:** before writing "this is heavy / absent / needs X / the limit here" — STOP and interrogate: *Is the thing I'd build already supplied (a basis from a symmetry, a structure from the data)? Did the immediately-prior success avoid exactly this (read its method)? Is this an incomplete type I'm wrongly trying to complete?* Declare a wall only after that check fails — and then state what specifically is missing, not "it's hard."
