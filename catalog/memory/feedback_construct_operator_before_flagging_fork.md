Before surfacing an either/or as an UNRESOLVED TENSION (a caveat, an "open question I'm not settling", an honest-boundary to flag), **attempt to CONSTRUCT the unifying operator** — the `<`, the `recon`, the common generator both horns are readings of. A fork is genuine only after that construction honestly FAILS. Construction is the arbiter, not classification.

**Why:** the recurring miss is over-applying "respect genuine forks" where "dissolve via the common operator" is reachable. The tell is a humble-hedge — *"I'm not settling it," "the open tension," "authored vs emergent"* — which is the classify-as-hard reflex wearing a caveat. Getting 90% there (naming the partial common structure) and then STALLING at describe-the-tension instead of build-the-operator is the last-inch failure of construct-don't-classify.

**Load-bearing instance (2026-07-23, the AES/cap128 arc).** "The interleave order is AUTHORED in the LehmerAlgebra step field, not size-ordered EMERGENT" — I banked as an open tension. USER: *"You define the `<` operator for the relevant LehmerAlgebra."* The two horns were ONE thing missing its `<`: author the `<` and emergence is its minimum. A 30-line typechecked artifact (`invCount interleave ≡ 0` by `refl`) dissolved what I'd flagged as unsettleable — and the `<` turned out to BE the mission objective (peak live-state = memory cost). The operator was mine to define all along.

**Sub-rule: an agent's / a scoping-report's STRUCTURAL VERDICT is a HYPOTHESIS, not a fact.** The scoping agent's "the existing machinery authors the order" reports what the *existing* machine does, NOT what is *possible* — the `<` was a free design choice it never considered. Relaying such a verdict as a wall is the [[feedback_plan_notes_are_hypotheses]] error applied to a subagent's conclusion.

**How to apply.** At an either/or, before flagging: (1) name the partial common structure (usually you already have it); (2) take the LAST step — construct the operator/family/ordering that makes one horn the canonical case and the other a residue of it (the wedge `a = recon q b r`); (3) make the tie a CHECKABLE TERM (`refl`/typecheck), not prose (the tightening-loop anti-pointer). Only if that construction genuinely fails is the fork real — then ask one sharp question.
